Bilingual Sutradhaar (सूत्रधार)

A discourse on storytelling, language, and meaning

An aspiring writer's run-in with Premchand's speeches to writer's society in late 1800s India.
Shubhra Prakash is a theatre artist who writes stories in English that have Hindi—and aspires to write plays in Hindi with English.

Terminology: transliteration 📝 — writing the original sounds of one language in another script (e.g., "कर्म" → "karma"). Translation — rendering meaning in another language. Literal translation — close to word-for-word, preserving structure.
