About the Project

FONTWALA is a solo and ensemble theatre piece inspired by the life and work of Rajeev Prakash — an Indian typographer and font designer whose legacy spans over 20 scripts and keyboard systems. It blends personal archive, migration, and the transformation of Indian languages across borders and platforms — from stone to mobile.
