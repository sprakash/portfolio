Fontwala

🎭 About the Play

In 1990s India, Fontwala is an aspiring typographer and font designer of Indian languages who designs the Anglo Nagari Keyboard, one of the first software that allows typing of Indian scripts on the Latin-based keyboard. In the present time, Shilpi, Fontwala’s niece, has returned to India after two decades in the US to write his story. Through her journey, the play explores memory, language, technology, and legacy.

👩‍🎤 About Shubhra

Shubhra Prakash is a writer and performer whose work bridges Indian cultural heritage and contemporary issues. She is the creator of Fontwala and co-author of Priya’s Mask, featured by NPR and BBC. Her theater and media projects seek to amplify voices that question the status quo.

🧠 About Fontwala (Rajeev Prakash)

Fontwala (Rajeev Prakash) began painting hand-lettered ads in India in the 1970s, later designing fonts for Devanagari scripts in the digital age. His innovations contributed to early multilingual computing in India.
