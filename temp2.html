About FONTWALA

FONTWALA is a hybrid, evolving theatre-project: a one-woman English-language solo play, a Hindi-language ensemble production, a digital exhibition, and a forthcoming documentary—built around the life and work of script-visionary Rajeev Prakash Khare (aka “Fontwala”).

class="w-full md:w-3/4 lg:w-1/2 h-auto mx-auto my-6 rounded-lg shadow-lg object-contain">

Beginning in 2017 when theatre-maker and designer Shubhra Prakash interviewed her uncle Rajeev, the story expands far beyond biography:

  • What happens when the artisan becomes entrepreneur?
  • What is lost—and what remains—of a script and language when it is digitised?
  • What does it mean to return home after decades in another world?

Two Versions. One Mission.

English Solo Play

A global, intimate journey—written and performed by Shubhra, directed by Marcus Yi—this version travels to festivals and theatres in the U.S. and India, engaging audiences who speak English and explore universal questions of language, migration, and craft.

Hindi Ensemble Play

Fully realised in Hindi (with elements of Bhojpuri, Bundelkhandi, and Marathi), this production dramatizes the community, dialects, and vernacular world that shaped Rajeev’s career, inviting Indian audiences into rich textures of story through collaboration and ensemble energy.

We are actively seeking partners: theatres, cultural centres, festivals, designers, calligraphers, typographers, digital storytellers, and collaborators for site-specific or multilingual adaptations.
