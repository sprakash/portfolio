📜 When Premchand Spoke to Writers

Premchand gave several speeches to writers in India during his lifetime. He spoke on the purpose of literature, the craft of writing, and the politics of language, particularly Hindi and Urdu. These speeches are collected in a Hindi book titled कुछ विचार (Kuch Vichar).

📘 You can read the original Hindi book here:
archive.org - Kuch Vichar by Premchand

📄 I have translated this book into English and made it available as a free downloadable PDF. This website is a result of that translation journey.
