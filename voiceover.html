🎙️ About Me

A trained theater artist and voiceover talent with experience across commercials, narration, and character work with voiceover specific training at San Francisco’s VoiceOne.

I’ve had recent repeat bookings from Google—for Pixel, GooglePay, and the WhereIsMyTrain app—voicing in both Hindi and English with an Indian accent. Recently voiced the role of Yash, a 12-year-old boy, in The Gods Have Returned (2025) by Voyage Media, and my Punjabi-language spot for Remitly is currently live on YouTube.

Having spent my formative years in India, I also provide language services in Hindi—offering script assistance, translation, and culturally grounded insight for teams looking to connect authentically with Hindi speaking audiences. Thanks to my home studio and representation with Artistic Talent, I audition and deliver broadcast-quality work reliably and can also record at studios in New York if needed.
